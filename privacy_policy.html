01Who we are

Walloo: 4K Wallpapers ("Walloo", "we", "us") is an Android app for browsing and setting wallpapers, published on Google Play under the package name com.walloo.wallpapper. The developer of Walloo is the data controller responsible for the personal data described here.

This policy covers the Walloo app and the Walloo web pages that link to it. Third-party services used by the app (for example Google, Meta and Unity) also have their own privacy policies, linked in section 12.

03Google sign-in, sync and Community

Signing in (optional)

You can sign in with your Google account through Firebase Authentication. We then receive your name, email address, profile photo link and a Google account identifier. We never see your Google password.

Favorites sync

When you are signed in, your favorite wallpapers and folders are saved in our cloud database (Google Cloud Firestore) so they are available after reinstalling or on another phone. What is saved is the list of wallpapers (their links, sizes and credits) and your folder names — not files from your phone.

Community collections

If you publish a collection, it becomes public: anyone using Walloo can see the collection name, the wallpapers in it, and your display name and profile photo as its curator. Others can like and save it. You can unpublish or delete your collections at any time, and they are deleted with your account.

If you never sign in, none of the above applies: nothing is linked to an account and your favorites stay only on your phone.

04Data collected automatically

Firebase Analytics (Google)

We record how the app is used, for example which screens are opened, which categories are browsed, when a wallpaper is set or downloaded, when a paywall or an ad is shown, and when the app crashes. Each event comes with an app-instance identifier, the device model, Android version, app version, language, approximate country (derived from the IP address) and session information. We also tag which version of an experiment you see (for example, which tab layout). We use this only in aggregate to improve Walloo. We do not link analytics to your name or email.

Firebase Crashlytics (Google)

If the app crashes or hits an error, a crash report is sent with the technical details: the error and stack trace, the device model, Android version, app version, memory and disk state, and an installation identifier. Crash reports do not contain your name or email.

Firebase Remote Config and Installations (Google)

The app downloads its settings (for example feature switches and how often ads appear) from Firebase. This uses a random installation identifier.

Google Play services

Google Play may check for app updates (In-App Updates) and may show its own rating dialog (In-App Review). Google handles these directly; we do not see your review or your Play account.

05What stays on your device

The following is stored only on your phone and is never sent to us. It is erased when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

  • Your settings, favorites (when not signed in), recently viewed wallpapers and download history.
  • A simple score of which categories you like, used to personalise your feed. It is calculated and kept on your phone only.
  • Your Auto Wallpaper schedule and queue, and a local library of wallpapers prepared for your feed.
  • Your Live wallpaper settings. Live wallpapers react to how you tilt your phone using the motion sensor. Sensor readings are used instantly to move the image and are never stored or sent anywhere.
  • Your Walloo Pro status, kept in encrypted storage.
  • Cached images, so wallpapers load faster and use less data.

Wallpapers you download are saved to your phone's Pictures folder. Wallpapers you set are applied by Android on your device.

06Advertising

The free version of Walloo is supported by ads: native ads in the wallpaper grids, full-screen ads between some actions, and optional rewarded ads you choose to watch (for example to unlock a download or one free Live wallpaper per day).

Who serves the ads

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC), which runs the ad auction.
  • Ad networks that compete in AdMob's auction (mediation): Meta Audience Network (Meta Platforms) and Unity Ads (Unity Technologies).
  • Other advertising partners that Google AdMob works with. The current list is shown in the consent message and on Google's ad technology providers page.

What they collect

To show, measure and protect ads, these providers may collect your device's Advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. From the IP address they may estimate your approximate location (country or city level, never precise GPS), used to show ads relevant to your region and to prevent fraud. With your consent this can be used for personalised ads; otherwise they show non-personalised or limited ads based on context.

Your choices

  • Consent: in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Walloo shows Google's consent message the first time you open the app, before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time in Walloo → Settings → Ad choices.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by phone).
  • No ads at all: Walloo Pro removes all ads.

Our ad requests are not marked as directed to children; see section 17.

07Purchases and Walloo Pro

Walloo Pro is sold as a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase, all through Google Play Billing. Google processes your payment; we never see your card or bank details.

The app receives from Google Play which product you bought, whether the purchase is active, and a purchase token. These stay on your phone and are used only to unlock Pro and to restore it. You manage or cancel subscriptions in the Google Play Store.

08Wallpaper images and their sources

Walloo's wallpapers come from three photo services: Unsplash, Pexels and Pixabay. Photographers are credited in the app.

  • Many feeds are prepared once a day on our server and delivered from our database; those requests do not involve your data.
  • When you search, scroll far, or open a photographer's page, your phone may request images directly from these services. Like any website, they receive your IP address and basic device information as part of the request. We do not send them your name, email or account.
  • Some image links expire and are refreshed automatically; this sends only the photo's ID.

When you share a wallpaper, Android's share menu opens with a link to the photo's source page; the app you choose then handles it.

09Notifications

If you allow notifications, Walloo uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to send a wallpaper of the day and occasional news about the app. This uses a device token and subscribes your phone to a general topic; it is not linked to your name or email. While the app is open, it may display these messages itself. You can turn notifications off in Walloo → Settings or in Android settings at any time.

15Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or California law), you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • delete your data;
  • restrict or object to processing, including processing based on our legitimate interests;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time (ads in Settings → Ad choices; notifications in Settings), without affecting earlier processing;
  • complain to your data protection authority. In Romania this is the ANSPDCP (dataprotection.ro).

To exercise these rights, email us at the address in section 20 from the email of the Google account you use in Walloo (if any). We reply within 30 days and do not charge for reasonable requests. We will never treat you differently for exercising your rights.

16Deleting your account and data

In the app

  1. Open Walloo → Settings.
  2. Tap your account card at the top.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This permanently deletes your Walloo account, your profile, your synced favorites and folders, and every collection you published. Likes you gave to other people's collections may remain as anonymous counts, no longer linked to any account.

Without the app

Use our account deletion page or email us. We delete your account and its data within 30 days.

Data on your phone

Everything stored only on your phone is removed when you clear Walloo's data in Android settings or uninstall the app. Analytics and crash data are not linked to your account and expire as described in section 13.

17Children

Walloo is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country, such as 16 in some EU countries). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

18Security

  • All network traffic uses encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Our cloud database uses security rules: only you can read or change your own account data, and only you can edit your collections.
  • Your Walloo Pro status is kept in encrypted storage on your phone.
  • Access to our Firebase and Google Cloud project is restricted to the developer.

No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information and will inform you and the authorities of a breach where the law requires it.

19Changes to this policy

When Walloo changes in a way that affects your data, we update this page and its effective date. For significant changes, we will also tell you in the app before they take effect. Earlier versions are available on request.
