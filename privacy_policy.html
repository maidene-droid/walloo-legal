2How We Use Your Data

  • To provide core features — search, display, and set wallpapers; favorites; auto-wallpaper scheduling.
  • To personalize content — show weather-appropriate wallpapers based on your approximate location (only when you grant permission).
  • To process purchases — verify and unlock Premium or Remove-Ads entitlements via Google Play.
  • To show ads — free-tier users see non-personalized or personalized ads through Google AdMob. Premium / Remove-Ads users see no ads.
  • To improve the App — aggregate analytics and crash data help us identify and fix issues and prioritize features.
  • To send notifications — optional push notifications about new collections or features (you can disable these at any time in device settings).
  • To comply with legal obligations — where required by applicable law.

We process your data on the following legal bases (GDPR Article 6):

  • Performance of a contract — to deliver the services you use (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Legitimate interests — analytics and crash reporting to maintain a functional, secure app (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Consent — location access, personalized advertising, and push notifications (Art. 6(1)(a)).

4Location Data

The App may request access to your device's location (precise or approximate) solely to power the Weather Walls feature, which displays wallpapers matching your current weather and time of day.

  • Location access is optional. The App functions fully without it.
  • Location is fetched on-demand only when the feature is active — it is not tracked continuously in the background.
  • Your coordinates are sent to the OpenWeatherMap API to retrieve current weather conditions. They are not stored on our servers.
  • You can revoke location permission at any time via Android Settings → Apps → Walloo → Permissions.

5Advertising

Free-tier users may see ads served by Google AdMob, including native ads and interstitial ads. AdMob may use your Advertising ID (GAID) and other signals to serve personalized or contextual ads.

  • You can opt out of personalized ads via Android Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • Users who purchase Premium or Remove Ads see no advertisements.
  • We comply with Google's AdMob policies and applicable data protection laws.

7Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

  • Right of access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right of rectification — request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right of erasure — request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Right to restrict processing — request that we limit how we use your data.
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to withdraw consent — revoke any consent you have given at any time (e.g., location, notifications) without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address in Section 10. We will respond within 30 days.

Deleting your account You can delete your Walloo account at any time from Settings → Account → Delete Account within the App. This will remove your authentication data from Firebase. Local data (favorites, settings) is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear app data.

8Children's Privacy

Walloo is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

9Security

  • No sensitive data stored in plaintext on device.

No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable precautions to protect your information.
