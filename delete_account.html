Delete your Walloo account

You can delete your account and its data at any time — instantly in the app, or by sending us a request from this page.

This page belongs to Walloo: 4K Wallpapers, the Android app published on Google Play under the package name com.walloo.wallpapper.

Fastest · instant

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Walloo → Settings.
  2. Tap your account card at the top.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately — no email and no waiting.

What happens

What is deleted and what is kept

Deleted
  • Your Walloo account (name, email, profile photo, account ID)
  • Your synced favorites and folders
  • Every collection you published in Community
Kept or expiring on its own
  • Likes you gave to others' collections — as anonymous counts, no longer linked to any account
  • Analytics (up to 14 months) and crash reports (about 90 days) — never linked to your account
  • Data on your phone — removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data

Purchases and subscriptions are managed by Google Play and are not cancelled by deleting your account — cancel a subscription in the Play Store. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.
